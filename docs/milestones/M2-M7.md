# Milestones 2–7 report: the full app

14 October 2026 · app version `0.2.0-preview`

**Status: built and automatically tested end to end. Not yet validated with a real person on a real camera.** Rep counting for every exercise is tested on synthetic movement only. Your real-camera scripts (at the end) are what turn "built" into "dependable".

## What you can try now

Install from the CI run (see "Getting the installer"), and Ready Up opens to first-run setup instead of Diagnostics:

1. **Setup (under a minute, every step skippable):** welcome, routine (focus minutes, exercise, reps), games (choose your Overwatch 2 or Marvel Rivals shortcut, or paste a Steam/Battle.net/Epic link, then test-launch it), camera.
2. **Home:** "Next up: focus." The cursor waits in the task field; Enter starts.
3. **Focus:** large measured timer; Space pauses; End early asks first. Sleep pauses it automatically. A Windows notification fires when it ends.
4. **Focus done:** "Did you finish *task*?" Yes / Partly / Not this time (keys 1–3). Your answer is what's recorded.
5. **Camera setup** (first time, then weekly): live checks for in frame, head visible, distance, angle and lighting, each with a specific hint, plus a dashed guide outline and the phone-camera guide.
6. **Move:** camera count, depth gauge, live feedback, −1/+1 (keys - and +), exercise switcher, and "Can't use the camera?" (O) for the override.
7. **Ready:** the lock-in moment (≤ 1.1 s, or a 0.35 s fade with reduced motion), an honest summary, and Launch (Enter).
8. **History:** 7-day strip, weekly totals (camera-counted reps only), session details with corrections, overrides, game launches, and manual match logging.
9. **Settings:** routine, games, camera, motion and sound (sound off by default), export history, delete everything. **Diagnostics** is in Settings or Ctrl+Shift+D.

The camera is on only during camera setup, Move and Diagnostics. The pose model warms up in the background so Move starts instantly.

## Bugs caught and fixed while building

1. **A CSS class collision made "Continue anyway" an invisible full-screen box.** The guide outline and the transparent "ghost" buttons both used the class `ghost`. Found by the scripted walkthrough.
2. **The screen-change focus helper stole focus** from the Launch button on the Ready screen. Found by the reduced-motion test.
3. **zod tried `eval`** in the sandboxed UI, which the CSP correctly blocked. Found by the zero-network test's CSP check. zod now runs without eval in the UI.
4. **Small labels on finished slots were below 4.5:1 contrast.** Found by axe-core.
5. **Crash recovery wasn't written back to disk,** so a second crash could have counted the same time twice. Found by the crash test.
6. **The "too far away" check was too strict** (40% of the frame). At the model's ~4 m limit a body in a plank spans about 30%.

## Deviations and honest notes

- **Exercise changes mid-Move restart the count** for the new exercise, so counts from different movements never mix.
- **"One person in view" isn't checked.** The model tracks a single person by design, and the setup screen doesn't yet warn about a second person.
- **Sit-ups are Beta.** Lying-down poses are where pose models are weakest.
- **Squat and sit-up thresholds are first guesses** from geometry (knee ≤ 100°, hip ≤ 75°). Expect them to change after your tests.
- **Corrections after a restart:** the camera count and corrections carry over into Move after a restart, and the counter starts its own count fresh.

## Getting the installer

Every push runs CI. On the run page (GitHub → Actions → CI → the latest run), the **ready-up-windows-installers** artifact holds both installers. They're unsigned: at SmartScreen choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## Your real-camera scripts (no recording needed)

For each exercise: open Diagnostics (Ctrl+Shift+D), choose the exercise in **Input**, place the camera as the setup screen says, then:

**Push-ups**
1. 10 full push-ups → expect 10.
2. 5 half push-ups → expect 0, "Go a little lower".
3. 5 standing arm curls → expect 0.
4. Walk out of frame mid-rep → that rep voided.

**Knee push-ups**
1. 10 knee push-ups → expect 10.
2. 3 full push-ups → expect 0, with "That looks like a push-up".

**Squats**
1. 10 full squats → expect 10.
2. 5 half squats → expect 0, "Sit a little deeper".
3. Bend over to pick something up 3 times → expect 0.

**Sit-ups (Beta)**
1. 10 full sit-ups → expect 10.
2. 5 crunches → expect 0, "Sit all the way up".

Then do **one full ready check** with a real focus session (set Focus to 5 min in Settings), your real game shortcut, and the override once.
